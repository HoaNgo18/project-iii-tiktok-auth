Key Features

Built for review-first workflows

Everything needed to support secure authorization, internal review, and controlled publishing automation across platforms.

Account Authorization

OAuth-based account connection that securely links your publishing accounts. Tokens are managed internally with automatic refresh and scope validation for each integrated platform.

App Verification

Required product pages and policy documents for platform review, including Privacy Policy and Terms of Service hosted on verified domains.

Manual Review First

Every piece of content goes through human approval by the project owner before any publishing action is triggered. Nothing goes live without explicit sign-off.

Approved Publishing

Once reviewed and approved, content is passed into the publishing workflow automatically — with scheduling, platform routing, and status tracking.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about how threads_city works

What is threads_city?
threads_city is an internal content workflow tool that handles account authorization, app verification, and approved publishing automation for social media platforms.
Which platforms are supported?
We currently integrate with TikTok, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), YouTube, YouTube Shorts, and Twitter / X. Additional platforms are added as platform APIs evolve.
Is content published automatically?
No. Every piece of content must be manually reviewed and approved by the project owner before it enters the publishing pipeline. Automation only kicks in after explicit approval.
How are my account credentials handled?
We use OAuth 2.0 for all platform connections. Your credentials are never stored — only scoped access tokens are kept, and they can be revoked at any time through the connected platform.
