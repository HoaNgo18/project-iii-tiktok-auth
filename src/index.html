What It Does

A controlled content workflow,
from draft to approval

threads_city is an internal tool that connects to your publishing account, routes content through manual review, and publishes only after explicit approval.

Secure Authorization

OAuth-based account connection that links only the authorized publishing account. Tokens are scoped, refreshable, and never shared.

Manual Review Workflow

Each piece of content is reviewed by the project owner before any publishing action. Nothing goes live without explicit human approval.

Controlled Publishing

Approved content enters the publishing pipeline with scheduling and platform routing. Publishing actions are logged and traceable.

Workflow

Source to publish, step by step

Every piece of content follows a controlled pipeline before reaching the connected platform.

1

Source Content Selected

Content source is identified and queued for processing within the internal tool.

2

Draft Generated

A draft version is produced and staged for review, including metadata and platform routing.

3

Admin Manual Review

The project owner inspects the draft. Content is approved or sent back for revision. Nothing proceeds without sign-off.

4

Account Authorization Verified

The connected account's authorization is validated. Scoped tokens are confirmed before any publishing action.

5

Approved Content Published

Once approved, content is uploaded to the connected platform as a draft or scheduled post under the authorized account.

Safeguards

Built with operational trust in mind

Content governance, access control, and data handling practices designed for review-first environments.

No Publishing Without Approval

Content must pass manual review by the project owner. There is no automatic publishing path.

Connected Account Only

The tool operates exclusively on the account that granted authorization. No cross-account access or impersonation.

Private Token Handling

OAuth tokens are stored privately by the project owner. Credentials are never logged, shared, or transmitted externally.

Review-First Workflow

Every publishing action requires a documented review step. No content bypasses the approval gate.

Authorization & Data

What authorization is used for,
and what it's not

Authorization is used to

  • Connect a single publishing account via OAuth
  • Verify account identity and permissions before publishing
  • Upload approved content as a draft or scheduled post
  • Maintain token validity through automatic refresh

Authorization is not used to

  • Access account data beyond publishing scopes
  • Publish content without explicit manual approval
  • Share, sell, or distribute any account information
  • Impersonate the account owner or act autonomously
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is threads_city?
threads_city is an internal content workflow tool for account authorization, manual content review, and approved publishing. It operates as a private workflow layer between content selection and platform publishing.
Is content published automatically?
No. Every piece of content must be manually reviewed and approved by the project owner before it enters the publishing pipeline. Automation only applies after explicit human approval.
What data does the tool access?
The tool accesses only the data required for publishing: basic account profile information (to confirm the connected account) and publishing permissions. It does not read messages, view private content, or access user data beyond its declared scopes.
Who can authorize the connected account?
Only the account owner can authorize the connection through the platform's native OAuth flow. Authorization can be revoked at any time through the connected platform's account settings.
How are credentials and tokens stored?
OAuth tokens are stored privately by the project owner within the internal tool infrastructure. Account passwords are never collected. Tokens are scoped, refreshable, and handled according to each platform's security requirements.
