threads_city

threads_city is an internal content workflow tool used for account authorization, app verification, and approved publishing automation.

Learn More Privacy Policy

Core Features

Everything needed to support secure authorization, internal review, and controlled publishing automation.

Secure Authorization

Supports OAuth-based account connection for the authorized publishing account.

App Verification

Provides the required product pages and policy documents for platform review.

Manual Review First

Content is reviewed and approved by the project owner before any publishing action is triggered.
